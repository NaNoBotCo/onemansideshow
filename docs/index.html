The acts

What the show runs on.

Act I · Access

A person stood there

Rides the city with a 360° camera, reads Thai-language and official sources, and knows the shop owners by name. A fact checked on the street is marked as one.

motdang.net/field

Act II · Taxonomy

The facts that decide

Taxonomy work at Quora. A shelf says what a place is; a facet says whether it is worth the walk: wifi, step-free, a cash machine, open all night.

215 shelves, Thai and English

Act III · Throughput

Thirty-five hours a city

Seven reference directories in seventy days, timed from first commit to live site: 53 hours, then 45, 45, 36, 35, 35, 35.

20 sites · 99,303 pages · 302,396 records

Act IV · Evidence

The warrant

Trained as an intelligence analyst. A fact shows how it is known: joined from a map point nearby, tagged, or seen by a person. Unknown shows as blank, not as “no”.

motdang.net/brief

Act V · Place and time

Near · now · soon

Certified in GIS. Pages answer from where the reader stands: nearest by road, open now, the air, the rain, and what is on this week.

motdang.net

Act VI · Machine readers

Read by machines

AI crawlers made 85% of motdang.net’s requests in a September week. The pages are built for them: an open licence, llms.txt, schema.org, a source on each field.

Uptake, the manual

Act VII · The assistant

Answers from your records

Answers in the asker’s language, cites the records it used, marks general knowledge “not verified”, and logs what it could not find so the directory grows.

ask.motdang.net · pike.motdang.net

Act VIII · Messaging

Messages on a calendar

Festival and event campaigns drafted to their lead times for LINE and email. They go out on the owner’s word.

As a U.S. diplomat: wrote an embassy’s consular emergency plan, worked evacuations

Act IX · The fleet

Lay minister to bots

Agents on schedules, one job each: a clinic-licence sweep, a Facebook reader, a source scout, a LINE desk, a field pipeline. Each open question goes to the owner with its options, costs and a date.
